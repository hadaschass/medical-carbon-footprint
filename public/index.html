    Method & assumptions

    • Scope: cradle-to-grave, GWP100 in kg CO2e, per functional unit (e.g. one glove). Reusable items are shown per use: production, transport and disposal are divided over the number of uses, plus reprocessing per use.
    • Materials: mass × cradle-to-gate factor. Manufacturing: mass × process energy (kWh/kg) × grid intensity of the manufacturing country.
    • Sterilisation: mass of product + packaging × method factor. Transport: tonne-km × mode factor.
    • End of life: waste-route factor per kg. Biogenic carbon (natural rubber, cotton, paper) is treated as climate-neutral when burned.
    • Factors are indicative secondary data (ICE, PlasticsEurope, DEFRA/DESNZ, IEA and published medical-device LCAs). Results are screening estimates (±30 %) for hotspot analysis and comparison, not for certified claims.
